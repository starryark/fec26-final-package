## How to read this log

* **Scores** are our own public Development results as returned by Codabench (S of each machine). S_map = 0.55 R²ψ + 0.10 (1 − D_LCFS) + 0.20 Cons is the map part of the MAST score, computed from the same returned per-term values; it is the metric of the map reads from Oct 12 on. Deltas in the decision column are computed from these returned values. They are scores of our own predictions, not MAST labels; this log contains no MAST label and no statistic of MAST labels.
* **Policy** (team decision 2026-10-08, under the Rule 3 feedback clause of 2026-10-01): Development scores and Detailed Results of our own submissions may be used to compare, tune and select MAST pipelines, and that use is stated here and in the methods report; probe submissions (constant, shifted or rescaled predictions) are banned. Every submitted MAST variant is a complete, physically motivated pipeline we would be willing to submit as final. No continuous constant is fitted to board numbers by repeated submissions, with two team-approved exceptions: the solenoid fraction f, and from 2026-10-13 single physical machine knobs, read one at a time as pre-declared genuine candidates. Choices before the policy (sub01–sub04) used no MAST board feedback.
* **Other feedback use.** Besides the summary scores, the per-term and per-scalar R² and the per-shot 'worst 20 shots' tables of our own entries' Detailed Results were used to locate failure classes (e.g. frames with the P2 coils parked) and to size hypotheses for later candidates. No constant was fitted to them.
* **Visibility.** The last submission counts on the board (Force_Last), so baselines and restores without a read are listed too.
* **Dev use** says which machine's read a submission carried. DIII-D and MAST are scored independently, so one submission can carry one read per machine; a half marked '= subNN' or 'repeated' is a file already scored and carries no read.
* **Pre-declared rule**: the rule as written before the read, with its place in the research log (`docs/research/FEC_COMPETITION_20261007.md`, line at generation time) or a linked document, and the first commit of that text in the team's private research repository (UTC). '†' marks a cite whose first commit is later than the submission (the text carries an earlier hand-written time); '*' marks a submission time taken from the log's submissions table because the registry holds only the date.
